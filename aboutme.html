Hey! I'm Aaron, a tech consultant based in San Francisco with a passion for making technology accessible and empowering people to use it confidently. Many of them are retirees learning to set up new devices and build real digital confidence.

Looking ahead, I'm driven by the intersection of technology, data, and finance. With a background in economics and hands-on experience across consulting and client services, I'm focused on roles where I can apply analytical thinking to real-world problems.
